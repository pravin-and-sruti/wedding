Wedding Rituals

Sunday, November 15

  • MORNINGKasi Yatra A fun tradition where the groom is persuaded to embrace marriage over asceticism.
  • MORNINGOonjal The couple sways on a decorative swing while elders ward off evil eyes and bless their union.
  • MORNINGKannigadhanam The sacred moment of entrusting the bride to the groom.
  • MORNINGMuhurtham Tying the Mangalsutra at the auspicious hour — the moment they officially become husband and wife.
  • EVENINGNalangu A playful post-wedding ritual filled with fun games, turmeric, and light banter.

What to wear

Nothing mandatory — wear whatever makes you comfortable. Here's a loose guide for each day's mood.

Bold & bright

Fun casuals for the engagement. For the reception, go bold — indo-western or suits, lehenga, designer sarees — bring your bold and bright outfit of the decade.

Traditional & comfy

Dhotis and sarees are preferred. Go for your comfy clothes, and make sure to carry loads of blessings and wishes.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, parking is available on site for guests arriving by their own vehicle.

What's the best way to reach the venue from outside Tiruchirappalli?

Fly into Tiruchirappalli Airport or arrive by train at Tiruchirappalli Junction, both well connected to major cities. From the city, a cab or auto to the venue is easiest — see the venue section above for the full address.

Is there a dress code?

Nothing mandatory, but see the "What to wear" section above for a colour and style guide for each event.

What's the weather like in Tiruchirappalli in November?

Warm and a little humid, typically ranging from the low-20s to low-30s°C (70s to high-80s°F), with a chance of rain. Light, breathable fabrics and an umbrella are a good idea.

Can I take photos during the ceremonies?

Yes, feel free to capture the celebrations — professional photographers will also be present throughout.

By when should I RSVP?

The sooner you tell us, the better we can spoil you — RSVP soon.
